Server Rules

Simple rules that keep IronVale fair and fun for everyone.

01

No Hacking

Client modifications that give unfair advantages (x-ray, kill-aura, fly, etc.) are strictly forbidden.

02

No Griefing

Do not destroy, steal from, or edit builds belonging to other players without permission.

03

No Toxicity

Keep chat and voice welcoming. Harassment, slurs, and targeted insults are not tolerated.

04

Respect Everyone

Treat players and staff with respect, regardless of skill level or experience.

05

No Exploits

Reporting bugs is welcome; abusing duplication or game-breaking exploits is not.

06

No Spam

Keep chat, commands, and builds free of spam or disruptive repetition.

Breaking these rules may result in a warning, mute, kick, or ban depending on severity.
